What we actually cover

Every insurer lists general liability. The difference is in the endorsements nobody thinks about until a Tuesday morning goes wrong. Here is what sits inside a standard Pristine policy for each trade, at no extra charge.

Machine shops & fabrication

CNC · manual · welding · sheet metal
  • Coolant and hydraulic escape, including floor remediation
  • Work in progress at customer value, not scrap value
  • Spindle and controller breakdown
  • Chip fire and metal dust cleanup
  • Faulty workmanship recall, 250,000
  • Rented crane and hoist liability

Bakeries & food production

Retail · wholesale · commissary kitchens
  • Spoilage from utility interruption, 20,000
  • Oven and proofer breakdown, parts and labor
  • Contamination and product withdrawal
  • Loss of a single anchor account, 90 days
  • Delivery vehicle contents
  • Health department closure, per diem

Trades on the road

Electrical · plumbing · HVAC · low voltage
  • Tools stolen from a locked van, no forced entry required
  • Damage to the part of the property you were working on
  • Apprentice injury, first dollar
  • Rekeying and lock replacement after a key loss
  • Additional insured, added same day
  • Permit bond errors

Three claims from last year

Names changed, numbers not. An adjuster who has stood in a shop like yours is on site within two business days, and every claim under 50,000 is decided by that same adjuster without a committee.
